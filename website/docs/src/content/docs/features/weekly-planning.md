title: Weekly Planning
description: A guided session that walks the family through deciding the week ahead — loose ends, the calendar, meals, tasks, goals and the kids — one step at a time.

Weekly Planning is the half hour where the family sits down and decides the week ahead.
Waffled walks you through it one step at a time: what's still open from last week, what's
on the calendar, who gets time with whom, what's for dinner, who's doing which chore, and
what each kid's week is about. 🗓️

The session owns almost no data of its own. Each step reads the modules you already use and
writes its decisions back into them — an event you add is a real calendar event, a chore you
hand out is a real chore on the Tasks board. The session only keeps a record of what the week
decided, and that record points at the live data rather than copying it.

## Turn the module on first

Weekly Planning is an **optional module and it's OFF by default**. An owner or admin turns it
on in **Settings → [Modules](/administration/modules/)**. Once it's on you get:

- a **Planning** page in the web app's nav
- a **Weekly Planning** tile at the top of the **Family** tab on iPhone
- a **Planning** page on iPad — pin it to the side rail, or find it under **More**
- a Weekly Planning card on the iPad's Today dashboard (see [Where it works](#where-it-works))

Turn the module back off and all of that disappears. Nothing the session decided is
touched — it lives in your calendar, chores, goals and meal plan, not in the module.

## Starting a session

The **Planning** page opens on a lobby: the session's day, how many steps it has, the ten
steps grouped by what they're for, and **Start the session**.

It plans **the week ahead** by default. If today is your household's first day of the week,
that's the week starting today; otherwise it's the next one. The arrows beside the week let
you plan a different week — any later one, or back as far as the current week, but never a
week that's already over. Each week gets its own session and its own record.

Inside the session, every step looks the same:

- the step's name and **the one question it asks** ("Who's doing what?")
- a counter like **3 of 9** — tap it for the agenda, where you can jump to any step
- **Skip this step** on the left of the footer, and the step's answer on the right —
  **All handled**, **Looks right**, **Accept**, **Handed out** — with the next step's name
  beside it

Skipping is a real answer. The record shows which steps you skipped on purpose.

**Leave for now** (in the header, and in the agenda) puts you back on the lobby with the
session kept exactly where it was, a button to resume it, and the week arrows. Leaving is
remembered on that device only, so someone else can pick the session up on another device at
the step the family reached. **Start this week over**, in the agenda, throws the session away
after asking first. It discards the session record only: events you added and chores you
handed out stay where they are.

## The ten steps

The lobby and the agenda group the steps into five parts. A step whose module is off is
skipped automatically and never counted — Family night needs Family Night, Goals needs Goals,
Meals needs Meals, and Tasks needs Chores. You can also switch any step off yourself (see
[Settings](#settings)).

### Intake

**1 · Loose ends** — *"Anything still open from last week?"* Two groups, worked through one
card at a time or all at once with **See all**:

- **Not done** — what's already in the app and still open: overdue chores, items left
  unchecked on your lists, rhythms past due, and habit goals that are short for the week.
  Each card says where it came from (Chore, List, Rhythm, Goal) and who already has it, so
  one with no name on it reads as up for grabs.
- **Parked** — notes somebody wrote down during the week that don't live anywhere else yet.
  You can park a new one from here too.

Each card gets sent on to the step that will deal with it — **Tasks**, **Calendar**, **Kids**
or **Goals** for something not done; **Make it a task** or **Put it on the calendar** for a
parked note. Sending something on writes nothing yet; it only decides where it comes up later
in the session, and **Undo** takes it back. Marking something done already, or dropping a
parked note, are the answers that act straight away.

**Which lists?** opens a switch per list, for the lists that are meant to stay open — a
someday list, a wishlist — so they stop coming up every week. Your grocery list is never asked
about, because it rebuilds itself from the meal plan.

### Frame the week

**2 · Calendar** — *"Here's your week. Anything missing?"* The real week, seven days. Tap a
day to add what's missing: a line, a time, and who it's for. It's an ordinary
[calendar](/features/calendar/) event.

**3 · Horizon scan** — *"Anything further out you should see now?"* The month view, so you
see what's coming before it arrives. **＋** on a day adds a real event. The bar underneath
parks a note instead — "we're going camping, we need to pack" — and you can tag it for one of
the steps still ahead of you, which will then open with it. A note with no tag stays on the
board for the Recap and next week's Loose ends. Everything parked shows under **Parked in
this session**, where **Edit** changes the wording, the tag, or both.

### Claim the good

**4 · Family night** — *"Accept the rotation, or change it?"* This week's
[Family Night](/features/family-night/): who the rotation suggests for each part. Tap a face to
pin a part for this week only, write what each part actually is ("charades, kids vs parents"),
and set an optional theme. You can get *this* week's gathering onto the calendar — as a new
event, or by pointing at something already on the week — or call the week off. Calling it off
leaves the standing weekly event alone.

**5 · Connection** — *"Who gets time with whom?"* Pairings the app can see from who's on
which events. Time you're already spending together counts: **Link a time** lists every event
on the week with both of you on it, whoever else is there, and lets you say that one *is* your
time. Nobody's event is edited. **Make a pairing** takes any two people and any time, and
offers the gaps your week actually left; what it makes is an ordinary calendar event.

**6 · Goals** — *"What's each group's focus this week?"* A tab per goal group — the family's
list, one per person, a private one. Pick the goal that group's week is about and it's
featured, so it turns up on Today and the Goals page. Picking nothing is a real answer, and a
goal you pinned yourself is never un-pinned. **＋ New goal for this week** opens the goal
editor over the session; the new goal comes back as that group's suggestion. Adding a goal to
a group that isn't just yours needs permission to manage goals.

### Run the household

**7 · Meals** — *"What's planned, and what's still open?"* The week's dinners in the same
seven columns, with each night's events above the dish, so you can see why Thursday is a bad
night to cook. Tap a night to pick a recipe or a saved plate, or build a new plate on the spot
with the [Meal Builder](/features/meals/). **✨ Plan the rest for me** opens the meal planner's
"Plan my week" review, narrowed to the empty nights — it never touches a night somebody
already decided, and only writes the nights you approve. Afterwards **Undo the three** (or
however many) takes back exactly what it wrote; a night changed by hand since is left alone,
and the step says so. It uses the AI provider you set up — bring your own model or key. With
Chores on, **Who's shopping?** turns the grocery trip into a real chore with a person and a
day.

**8 · Tasks** — *"Who's doing what?"* A column per person, with everything nobody has taken in
an **Up for grabs** strip across the top. Tap a face or drag a card to hand a chore over, or
leave it up for grabs. Tap a task's name — or the day beside it — to open the chore editor and
fix the title, the stars or the day. Each column shows what that person already carries.

**9 · Kids** — *"What's your week about?"* The step the children can read. A card per child
with their week and their stars, and two questions: **one thing to focus on** (a goal they're
behind on, an overdue chore, or something in their own words) and **something to look forward
to** (picked off their own calendar). Only members set up as a kid get a card — teens are left
out on purpose. The focus also shows on that child's Family profile all week as **This week's
one thing**.

### Close

**10 · Recap** — *"Here's the week you just decided."* The week read back: what the session
changed, grouped by where each decision lives (Calendar, Meals, Chores, Goals, Family Night,
Kids); **Still on the board** — a last call on notes nobody picked up, with how long they've
waited; and **Left alone on purpose**. **Save the week** finishes the session.

### Notes you parked for a step

A note tagged for a step turns up in a box at the top of that step, together with anything
Loose ends sent there. Each note offers **Edit**, **Handled** and **Drop it**. Tasks and
Calendar also offer their own verb — **Make a task** or **Make an event** — which opens that
step's usual editor with the note's words filled in (**Handled** then reads **Already
handled**); the note is only ticked off if you actually save. Walk past a note and it stays
parked.

## The saved week

A finished session opens as **The week is decided**: each day with its dinner and events, what
changed grouped by module, the notes nobody tagged, and what was left alone on purpose. Under
that is the step-by-step list, which is where skipped steps are recorded. On the web, a line
for a calendar, meals, tasks or goals decision opens that page.

**Reopen the session** takes you back in to change something. The week arrows let you plan
another week, and **Start this week over** is here too.

## Where it works

| Surface | Support |
|---|---|
| Web / Kiosk | ✅ |
| iPhone | ✅ |
| iPad | ✅ |

The whole session — the lobby, all ten steps, the agenda, parked notes, the Recap and the
saved week — works on every surface. What differs is how you get to it:

- **Web / Kiosk** — **Planning** in the nav. Every step has its own address, so a refresh,
  the back button or a pasted link lands you on the step you were on.
- **iPhone** — the **Weekly Planning** tile in the **Family** tab. The bottom bar is full, so
  there's no planning tab, and the iPhone's Today has no planning card.
- **iPad** — a **Planning** page: pin it to the rail, or open it from **More**. The iPad's
  Today dashboard also gets a card: on the session day it says the session is due, while a
  week is part-planned it shows how many steps are decided with a progress bar, and once the
  week is saved it says so. Tap it to open the session.

The web app's Today page has no planning card.

## Settings

On the web the settings sit under the module's switch in **Settings → Modules → Weekly
Planning**. On iPhone and iPad they're under **Settings → Weekly Planning**, which configures
the session but doesn't start one.

- **Session happens on** — a day and a time, default **Sunday, 5:00 PM**. This is when to
  nudge the family to sit down. It doesn't change which week gets planned: that always follows
  your household's first day of the week (**Settings → Family & People → Week starts on**).
- **Show on the family display's Today** — the iPad Today card above (default **on**).
- **Steps** — a switch per step. Turn off anything your family doesn't do; the session skips
  it and never counts it. Steps whose module is off aren't offered, and are named instead.
- **Lists it asks about** — the same per-list switches as **Which lists?** in Loose ends.

## Module

Weekly Planning is an **optional module** (`weeklyPlanning`, default **OFF** — opt-in),
toggled in **Settings → Modules** by an owner or admin.

Anyone signed in can run a session. The session's day, time, Today card and steps are
admin-only. Which lists Loose ends asks about is the **set up planning** permission, which
every adult has by default and an admin can give a teen in **Settings → Family & People →
Permissions** — see [Permissions & roles](/concepts/permissions/).

## Notes

- 🔗 **Nothing is copied.** Every decision is a real event, chore, goal or meal in the module
  that owns it, and the record points at it. Change the event later and the record agrees.
- 🗑️ **Starting over is safe.** It throws away the session and its record, never what the
  session already put on the calendar, the Tasks board or the meal plan.
- 🧒 **Kids are members with the kid type.** With no kids in the household, the Kids step has
  nothing to ask — skip it, or switch it off in Settings.
- 🌐 **Needs a connection.** The session reads and writes through your server, like Chores and
  Rhythms; it doesn't work offline on the phone.
